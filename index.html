Data sources

OSM live on request
OpenStreetMap · Overpass API Live OSM tree nodes, tree rows, hedges, orchards and mapped green areas inside the campus bounding box. Open OpenStreetMap · ODbL
Bavarian Surveying Administration · DOP40 Official open 40 cm orthophotos provide the satellite-style map background. Open source · CC BY 4.0
Regional harvest calendar Typical German harvest windows are based on public seasonal calendars and adapted as approximate Würzburg guidance. Open seasonal calendar
mundraub.org · community mapping 17 fruit-tree locations inside the campus boundary: 8 apple, 7 cherry, 1 pear and 1 mirabelle. Verify access and ownership on site. Open original Mundraub map
Stadt Würzburg · Fachabteilung Gartenamt 56 municipal public-tree records around the campus. None of these city records lies inside the drawn university boundary. Open dataset · DL-DE-BY-2.0
